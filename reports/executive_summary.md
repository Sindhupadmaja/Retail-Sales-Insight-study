# Executive summary

## Growth
Revenue grew **138%** in January–August 2018 versus the same months of 2017,
driven almost entirely by order volume (+137%) rather than basket size.
The single biggest day was Black Friday, 2017-11-24, with 1,166 orders,
4.7× the average day in November 2017.

## Customer experience is the main risk
8.0% of delivered orders arrived after the promised date. Those orders averaged
**2.57 stars versus 4.29** for on-time orders, and
54% of them received a 1–2 star review (vs 9%). The gap holds
within states (-1.62 stars after controlling for geography), so it is not explained
by regional differences. Late rates are highest in CE (15%), BA (14%), RJ (13%).

## Retention is very low
Only **3.0%** of 94,983 customers ordered more than once, so growth
depends on acquiring new customers.

## Concentration
The top 8 of 74 categories generate half of revenue, led by
health beauty, watches gifts, bed bath table.

## Recommendations
1. Set delivery promises from actual carrier performance in high-late-rate states, and alert
   customers proactively when an order is at risk of missing its date.
2. Launch a second-purchase program (for example, post-delivery offers to customers whose order
   arrived on time and was rated 4–5 stars), since repeat buying is almost absent.
3. Plan carrier capacity ahead of Black Friday: in November 2017 the late rate rose to
   14% (from 4% on average in January–October) and the
   average review fell to 3.95, the lowest of the year.
